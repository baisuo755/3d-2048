Grove

3D 2048

Slide trees across the floating island. Two identical trees merge and grow into the next tier. Grow the World Tree to win.

How to play

  • Move: Arrow keys, WASD, touch swipe, or the on-screen d-pad. Moves follow your camera angle (up pushes away from you).
  • Camera: Drag to orbit, wheel or pinch to zoom. Hover any tree to see its name.
  • Rules: Identical trees merge once per move. A move that changes nothing does not spend a turn.
