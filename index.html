Grove

3D 2048

Slide trees across the floating island. Two identical trees merge and grow into the next tier. Grow the World Tree to win.

How to play

  • Touch: Swipe anywhere with one finger to slide trees; drag or pinch with two fingers to orbit and zoom. Toggle Orbit for one-handed camera rotation, or hold the d-pad to repeat moves.
  • Desktop: Arrow keys or WASD slide trees; drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. Hover (or tap on touch) any tree to see its name.
  • Rules: Moves follow your camera angle (up pushes away from you). Identical trees merge once per move; no-change moves do not spend a turn.
